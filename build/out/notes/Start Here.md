## How answering works

- **MCQ:** click the option, then give honest confidence. A lucky guess gets re-checked, so saying "Guess" helps you.
- **Numbers:** type the value and unit, then `~` and your confidence from 1 (guess) to 4 (certain), e.g. `9.8 m s-2 ~3`.
- **"Don't know"** is a valid answer and gives a better diagnosis than a random pick.
- **Diagrams:** keep `Question Sheets/Current.md` open in an Obsidian side pane. It updates live with every open question and its figure.
- **New chat per session:** this saves usage. All progress is kept in this folder.
