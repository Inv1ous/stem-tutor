# 12.1 Nitrogen and sulfur
> [!abstract] In one breath
> Nitrogen's strong, non-polar triple bond makes it comparatively unreactive. Yet nitrogen oxides formed at high temperature have important atmospheric effects, while ammonia readily accepts protons.

## Key ideas

Two nitrogen atoms share three electron pairs in $\ce{N2}$. The $\ce{N#N}$ triple bond has high bond energy, so breaking it requires substantial energy. The molecule is non-polar because the atoms have identical electronegativity. Both the bond strength and lack of polarity help explain its low reactivity under ordinary conditions. Do not claim the nitrogen atoms lack lone pairs: each atom has one.

A Brønsted–Lowry base accepts a proton. Ammonia uses its nitrogen lone pair to accept $\ce{H+}$, forming $\ce{NH4+}$: $\ce{NH3 + H+ -> NH4+}$. The new N–H bond is initially described as dative covalent because both bonding electrons come from nitrogen. The ammonium ion is tetrahedral, with an H–N–H angle of about $109.5^\circ$. In water, $\ce{NH3 + H2O <=> NH4+ + OH-}$; this equilibrium explains aqueous ammonia's basicity. The ammonium ion can donate a proton and so acts as a weak acid. Alkali displaces ammonia from an ammonium salt: $\ce{NH4+ + OH- -> NH3 + H2O}$. Warming an ammonium salt with aqueous sodium hydroxide releases ammonia gas, which turns damp red litmus blue. Adding an acid instead favours ammonium formation.

Lightning is a natural source of nitrogen oxides: electrical discharges give nitrogen and oxygen enough energy to react. Internal combustion engines are a man-made source because their high temperatures allow $\ce{N2 + O2 -> 2NO}$; NO is oxidised to $\ce{NO2}$. A catalytic converter reduces nitrogen oxides to nitrogen, while oxidising carbon monoxide to carbon dioxide and unburnt hydrocarbons to carbon dioxide and water. For example, $\ce{2NO + 2CO -> N2 + 2CO2}$. State which pollutant is oxidised and which is reduced.

In air, NO and $\ce{NO2}$ can react with unburnt hydrocarbons, under sunlight, to form peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN), a component of photochemical smog. The hydrocarbon and nitrogen oxide inputs matter: carbon monoxide alone is not the stated route to PAN. These are consequences of exhaust escaping removal.

Nitrogen oxides also contribute to acid rain directly through formation of nitric acid. For example, $\ce{4NO2 + O2 + 2H2O -> 4HNO3}$. They also catalyse oxidation of atmospheric sulfur dioxide: $\ce{NO2 + SO2 -> NO + SO3}$, then $\ce{2NO + O2 -> 2NO2}$. The regenerated $\ce{NO2}$ can react again. Finally, $\ce{SO3 + H2O -> H2SO4}$ contributes sulfuric acid to rain. Explain both the direct nitric acid route and the catalytic sulfuric acid route when asked about the role of nitrogen oxides.

## Method

1. For converter mole questions, write the balanced equation and calculate the limiting reactant.
2. Use its coefficient to find the amounts of *all* gaseous products, then add their amounts.
3. At r.t.p., multiply total moles by $24.0\,\mathrm{dm^3\,mol^{-1}}$.

> [!example]- Worked example
> For 7.5 g NO and 7.0 g CO, each reactant is 0.25 mol. In $\ce{2NO + 2CO -> N2 + 2CO2}$, the products are 0.125 mol $\ce{N2}$ and 0.25 mol $\ce{CO2}$.
> Total volume $=(0.125+0.25)\times24.0=9.0\,\mathrm{dm^3}$ at r.t.p.

## Traps

- **Trap:** A strong acid liberates ammonia from ammonium chloride. **Why it's wrong:** $\ce{NH4+}$ must lose a proton. **Instead:** Use an alkali to accept it.
- **Trap:** The converter oxidises nitrogen oxides. **Why it's wrong:** They are reduced to nitrogen. **Instead:** Assign oxidation and reduction to the correct pollutants.
- **Trap:** $\ce{NO2}$ is used up as it oxidises $\ce{SO2}$. **Why it's wrong:** Oxygen regenerates it from NO. **Instead:** Write both catalytic steps.

## Exam technique

For **explain**, link the chemical property to the consequence: strong non-polar bond to low nitrogen reactivity; high engine temperature to NO formation. For **describe** or **state**, name the exact source, pollutant, or product. In acid-rain answers, distinguish nitric acid formed directly from sulfuric acid made after NOx-catalysed oxidation of $\ce{SO2}$. Include equations and balance atoms when asked to show the catalytic cycle.
