## KCs

### P2-8.1 — Evaluating definite integrals (procedural)
Statement: Evaluation of definite integrals.
Prerequisites: P1-5.2 Integrating powers of x
Glossary: definite integral, limits of integration, evaluating integrals, fundamental theorem of calculus

### P2-8.2 — Area under a curve (procedural)
Statement: Interpretation of the definite integral as the area under a curve.
Guidance: Students will be expected to evaluate the area of a region bounded by a curve and given straight lines. For example, find the finite area bounded by the curve $y=6x-x^2$ and the line $y=2x$. $\int x \, dy$ will not be required. Students will be expected to be able to evaluate the area of a region bounded by two curves.
Prerequisites: P2-8.1 Evaluating definite integrals
Glossary: area under a curve, area between two curves, definite integral as area, bounded region

### P2-8.3 — Trapezium rule (procedural)
Statement: Approximation of area under a curve using the trapezium rule.
Guidance: For example, use the trapezium rule to approximate $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \sqrt{2x+1}\,dx$ using four strips. Use of an increasing number of trapezia to improve accuracy, and an estimate of the error, may be required.
Prerequisites: P2-8.1 Evaluating definite integrals
Glossary: trapezium rule, numerical integration, strips, estimating area, percentage error
