## KCs

### 9701-1.2.1 — Definition of isotope (factual)
Statement: define the term isotope in terms of numbers of protons and neutrons
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.3 Atomic, proton, mass and nucleon number
Glossary: isotope, same protons, different neutrons

### 9701-1.2.2 — Isotope nuclide notation (factual)
Statement: understand the notation $^{x}_{y}\mathrm{A}$ for isotopes, where $x$ is the mass or nucleon number and $y$ is the atomic or proton number
Prerequisites: 9701-1.2.1 Definition of isotope
Glossary: nuclide notation, mass number, atomic number, isotope symbol

### 9701-1.2.3 — Isotopes and chemical properties (conceptual)
Statement: state that and explain why isotopes of the same element have the same chemical properties
Prerequisites: 9701-1.2.1 Definition of isotope
Glossary: isotope, same electron configuration, chemical properties

### 9701-1.2.4 — Isotopes and physical properties (conceptual)
Statement: state that and explain why isotopes of the same element have different physical properties, limited to mass and density
Prerequisites: 9701-1.2.1 Definition of isotope
Glossary: isotope, mass, density, physical properties
