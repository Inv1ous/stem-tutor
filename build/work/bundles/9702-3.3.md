## KCs

### 9702-3.3.1 — Conservation of momentum (factual)
Statement: state the principle of conservation of momentum
Prerequisites: 9702-3.1.3 Defining linear momentum
Glossary: conservation of momentum, principle of conservation of momentum, closed system

### 9702-3.3.2 — Solving collision problems with momentum (procedural)
Statement: apply the principle of conservation of momentum to solve simple problems, including elastic and inelastic interactions between objects in both one and two dimensions (knowledge of the concept of coefficient of restitution is not required)
Prerequisites: 9702-3.3.1 Conservation of momentum; 9702-1.4.3 Resolving a vector into components
Glossary: elastic collision, inelastic collision, conservation of momentum, one and two dimensions

### 9702-3.3.3 — Elastic collisions and kinetic energy (factual)
Statement: recall that, for an elastic collision, total kinetic energy is conserved and the relative speed of approach is equal to the relative speed of separation
Prerequisites: 9702-3.3.1 Conservation of momentum; 9702-3.3.2 Solving collision problems with momentum
Glossary: elastic collision, relative speed of approach, relative speed of separation, kinetic energy

### 9702-3.3.4 — Momentum conserved, kinetic energy may change (conceptual)
Statement: understand that, while momentum of a system is always conserved in interactions between objects, some change in kinetic energy may take place
Prerequisites: 9702-3.3.1 Conservation of momentum; 9702-3.3.3 Elastic collisions and kinetic energy
Glossary: conservation of momentum, kinetic energy loss, inelastic collision
