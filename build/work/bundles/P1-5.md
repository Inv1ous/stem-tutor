## KCs

### P1-5.1 — Integration as reverse of differentiation (conceptual)
Statement: Indefinite integration as the reverse of differentiation.
Guidance: Students should know that a constant of integration is required.
Prerequisites: P1-4.2 Differentiating powers of x
Glossary: indefinite integration, constant of integration, antiderivative, reverse of differentiation

### P1-5.2 — Integrating powers of x (procedural)
Statement: Integration of $x^n$ and related sums, differences and constant multiples.
Guidance: (Excluding $n=-1$, and related sums, differences and multiples). For example, the ability to integrate expressions such as $(x+2)^2$ and $\dfrac{3x-1}{2x^2}$ is expected. Given $f'(x)$ and a point on the curve, students should be able to find an equation of the curve in the form $y=f(x)$.
Prerequisites: P1-5.1 Integration as reverse of differentiation
Glossary: integrating x^n, constant of integration, finding equation of a curve, power rule for integration

## Command words

- **Show that**: Prove a given result; every step must be shown (answer given).
- **Hence**: Use the previous result to reach the answer.
- **Hence or otherwise**: Using the previous result is expected, but any valid method earns the marks.
- **Find**: Work out; method marks need working.
- **Calculate**: Work out from given facts; show working.
- **Prove**: Give a complete logical argument.
- **Sketch**: Draw the shape with key features labelled (intercepts, asymptotes, turning points), not to scale.
- **Exact**: Give the answer in surd, fraction, pi or ln form, not a decimal.
- **Verify**: Show that given values satisfy the statement.
- **State**: Give the answer without working.
- **Explain**: Give reasons in context.
- **Interpret**: Explain the meaning of a result in the context of the question.
