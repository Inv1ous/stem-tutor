## KCs

### S1-4.1 — Linear regression by least squares (procedural)
Statement: Scatter diagrams. Linear regression. Calculation of the equation of a linear regression line using the method of least squares.
Guidance: Students may be required to draw this regression line on a scatter diagram.
Prerequisites: S1-2.2 Mean, median and mode
Glossary: scatter diagram, linear regression, least squares, regression line

### S1-4.2 — Explanatory and response variables (conceptual)
Statement: Explanatory (independent) and response (dependent) variables. Applications and interpretations.
Guidance: Use to make predictions within the range of values of the explanatory variable and the dangers of extrapolation. Derivations will not be required. Variables other than $x$ and $y$ may be used. Linear change of variable may be required.
Prerequisites: S1-4.1 Linear regression by least squares
Glossary: explanatory variable, response variable, independent variable, dependent variable, extrapolation

### S1-4.3 — Product moment correlation coefficient (conceptual)
Statement: The product moment correlation coefficient, its use, interpretation and limitations.
Guidance: Derivations and tests of significance will not be required.
Prerequisites: S1-4.1 Linear regression by least squares
Glossary: product moment correlation coefficient, pmcc, correlation, linear association

## Command words

- **Show that**: Prove a given result; every step must be shown (answer given).
- **Hence**: Use the previous result to reach the answer.
- **Hence or otherwise**: Using the previous result is expected, but any valid method earns the marks.
- **Find**: Work out; method marks need working.
- **Calculate**: Work out from given facts; show working.
- **Prove**: Give a complete logical argument.
- **Sketch**: Draw the shape with key features labelled (intercepts, asymptotes, turning points), not to scale.
- **Exact**: Give the answer in surd, fraction, pi or ln form, not a decimal.
- **Verify**: Show that given values satisfy the statement.
- **State**: Give the answer without working.
- **Explain**: Give reasons in context.
- **Interpret**: Explain the meaning of a result in the context of the question.
