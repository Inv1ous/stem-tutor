## KCs

### 9701-3.2.1 — Definition of ionic bonding (factual)
Statement: define ionic bonding as the electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions (positively charged cations and negatively charged anions)
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.1 Definition of electronegativity
Glossary: ionic bonding, cation, anion, electrostatic attraction

### 9701-3.2.2 — Describing ionic bonding in NaCl, MgO, CaF2 (factual)
Statement: describe ionic bonding including the examples of sodium chloride, magnesium oxide and calcium fluoride
Prerequisites: 9701-3.2.1 Definition of ionic bonding
Glossary: sodium chloride, magnesium oxide, calcium fluoride, giant ionic lattice
