## KCs

### 9701-4.1.1 — Origin of gas pressure (conceptual)
Statement: explain the origin of pressure in a gas in terms of collisions between gas molecules and the wall of the container
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.1 Atomic structure: nucleus and electron shells
Glossary: gas pressure, molecular collisions, kinetic theory

### 9701-4.1.2 — Properties of an ideal gas (factual)
Statement: understand that ideal gases have zero particle volume and no intermolecular forces of attraction
Prerequisites: 9701-4.1.1 Origin of gas pressure
Glossary: ideal gas, zero particle volume, no intermolecular forces

### 9701-4.1.3 — Ideal gas equation pV = nRT (procedural)
Statement: state and use the ideal gas equation $pV = nRT$ in calculations, including in the determination of $M_r$
Prerequisites: 9701-4.1.2 Properties of an ideal gas; 9701-2.2.1 The mole and the Avogadro constant
Glossary: ideal gas equation, pv=nrt, molar gas constant, determination of mr
