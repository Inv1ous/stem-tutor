## KCs

### 9701-4.2.1 — Lattice structures of crystalline solids (factual)
Statement: describe, in simple terms, the lattice structure of a crystalline solid which is: (a) giant ionic, including sodium chloride and magnesium oxide (b) simple molecular, including iodine, buckminsterfullerene $\ce{C60}$ and ice (c) giant molecular, including silicon(IV) oxide, graphite and diamond (d) giant metallic, including copper
Prerequisites: 9701-3.2.1 Definition of ionic bonding; 9701-3.3.1 Definition of metallic bonding; 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: giant ionic lattice, simple molecular lattice, giant molecular lattice, giant metallic lattice

### 9701-4.2.2 — Structure, bonding and physical properties (conceptual)
Statement: describe, interpret and predict the effect of different types of structure and bonding on the physical properties of substances, including melting point, boiling point, electrical conductivity and solubility
Prerequisites: 9701-4.2.1 Lattice structures of crystalline solids; 9701-3.6.3 Van der Waals' forces: id-id and pd-pd
Glossary: melting point, boiling point, electrical conductivity, solubility, structure and bonding

### 9701-4.2.3 — Deducing structure and bonding from data (procedural)
Statement: deduce the type of structure and bonding present in a substance from given information
Prerequisites: 9701-4.2.2 Structure, bonding and physical properties
Glossary: structure deduction, bonding deduction, physical property evidence
