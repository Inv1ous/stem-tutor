## KCs

### 9701-1.4.1 — Definition of first ionisation energy (factual)
Statement: define and use the term first ionisation energy, $IE_1$
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.4 Writing electronic configurations
Glossary: first ionisation energy, gaseous atom, one mole, ie1

### 9701-1.4.2 — Equations for successive ionisation energies (procedural)
Statement: construct equations to represent first, second and subsequent ionisation energies
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.1 Definition of first ionisation energy
Glossary: ionisation equation, state symbols, successive ionisation energy

### 9701-1.4.3 — Trends in ionisation energy across period and group (conceptual)
Statement: identify and explain the trends in ionisation energies across a period and down a group of the Periodic Table
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.1 Definition of first ionisation energy; 9701-1.1.3 Atomic, proton, mass and nucleon number
Glossary: ionisation energy trend, period trend, group trend, nuclear charge, shielding

### 9701-1.4.4 — Successive ionisation energies of an element (conceptual)
Statement: identify and explain the variation in successive ionisation energies of an element
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.2 Equations for successive ionisation energies; 9701-1.3.4 Writing electronic configurations
Glossary: successive ionisation energy, big jump, shell structure, log ie graph

### 9701-1.4.5 — Ionisation energy and nuclear attraction (conceptual)
Statement: understand that ionisation energies are due to the attraction between the nucleus and the outer electron
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.1 Definition of first ionisation energy
Glossary: nuclear attraction, outer electron, electrostatic attraction

### 9701-1.4.6 — Factors affecting ionisation energy (conceptual)
Statement: explain the factors influencing the ionisation energies of elements in terms of nuclear charge, atomic/ionic radius, shielding by inner shells and sub-shells and spin-pair repulsion
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.5 Ionisation energy and nuclear attraction; 9701-1.3.4 Writing electronic configurations
Glossary: nuclear charge, shielding, atomic radius, spin-pair repulsion

### 9701-1.4.7 — Deducing electron configuration from ionisation data (procedural)
Statement: deduce the electronic configurations of elements using successive ionisation energy data
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.4 Successive ionisation energies of an element
Glossary: successive ionisation energy, electron configuration, shell breaks

### 9701-1.4.8 — Deducing periodic table position from ionisation data (procedural)
Statement: deduce the position of an element in the Periodic Table using successive ionisation energy data
Prerequisites: 9701-1.4.7 Deducing electron configuration from ionisation data
Glossary: periodic table position, group number, successive ionisation energy
