## KCs

### 9702-7.1.1 — Describing wave motion (factual)
Statement: describe what is meant by wave motion as illustrated by vibration in ropes, springs and ripple tanks
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: wave motion, vibration, ripple tank

### 9702-7.1.2 — Wave terms: amplitude, period, wavelength (factual)
Statement: understand and use the terms displacement, amplitude, phase difference, period, frequency, wavelength and speed
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.1 Describing wave motion
Glossary: displacement, amplitude, phase difference, period, frequency, wavelength

### 9702-7.1.3 — Using a CRO for frequency and amplitude (procedural)
Statement: understand the use of the time-base and y-gain of a cathode-ray oscilloscope (CRO) to determine frequency and amplitude
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.2 Wave terms: amplitude, period, wavelength
Glossary: cathode-ray oscilloscope, time-base, y-gain, cro

### 9702-7.1.4 — Deriving the wave equation (procedural)
Statement: derive, using the definitions of speed, frequency and wavelength, the wave equation $v = f\lambda$
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.2 Wave terms: amplitude, period, wavelength
Glossary: wave equation, v = f lambda, speed frequency wavelength

### 9702-7.1.5 — Using the wave equation, v = fλ (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $v = f\lambda$
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.4 Deriving the wave equation
Glossary: wave equation, v = f lambda, frequency and wavelength

### 9702-7.1.6 — Energy transfer by a progressive wave (conceptual)
Statement: understand that energy is transferred by a progressive wave
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.1 Describing wave motion
Glossary: energy transfer, progressive wave, wave energy

### 9702-7.1.7 — Wave intensity and amplitude (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $\text{intensity} = \text{power}/\text{area}$ and $\text{intensity} \propto (\text{amplitude})^2$ for a progressive wave
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.6 Energy transfer by a progressive wave; 9702-5.1.5 Defining power
Glossary: intensity, power per unit area, intensity proportional to amplitude squared

- **ER 9702 s22 P13 Q23** (matched: amplitude, displacement, period, time-base, y-gain): The amplitude of a sound wave is the maximum displacement from the equilibrium position, so its unit is m. The y-gain scale of the CRO is in units of V div–1. Using the amplitude of the CRO trace to determine the amplitude of the sound wave involves a calculation converting V to m, and this would require knowledge of the behaviour of the microphone. The time-base scale of the CRO is in units of s (or some multiple), so a direct reading of a single wave cycle on the horizontal axis of the CRO display would give the period of the wave.
