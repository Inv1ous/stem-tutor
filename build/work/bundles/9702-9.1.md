## KCs

### 9702-9.1.1 — Electric current as charge flow (conceptual)
Statement: understand that an electric current is a flow of charge carriers
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: electric current, charge carriers, flow of charge

### 9702-9.1.2 — Quantisation of charge (conceptual)
Statement: understand that the charge on charge carriers is quantised
Prerequisites: 9702-9.1.1 Electric current as charge flow
Glossary: quantised charge, elementary charge, charge carriers

### 9702-9.1.3 — Charge, current and time, Q = It (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $Q = It$
Prerequisites: 9702-9.1.1 Electric current as charge flow
Glossary: charge, q = it, current and time

### 9702-9.1.4 — Current in terms of charge carriers (procedural)
Statement: use, for a current-carrying conductor, the expression $I = Anvq$, where $n$ is the number density of charge carriers
Prerequisites: 9702-9.1.3 Charge, current and time, Q = It; 9702-9.1.2 Quantisation of charge
Glossary: number density of charge carriers, i = anvq, drift velocity
