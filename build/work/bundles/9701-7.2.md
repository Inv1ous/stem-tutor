## KCs

### 9701-7.2.1 — Common acids: names and formulas (factual)
Statement: state the names and formulas of the common acids, limited to hydrochloric acid, $\ce{HCl}$, sulfuric acid, $\ce{H2SO4}$, nitric acid, $\ce{HNO3}$, ethanoic acid, $\ce{CH3COOH}$
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: hydrochloric acid, sulfuric acid, nitric acid, ethanoic acid

### 9701-7.2.2 — Common alkalis: names and formulas (factual)
Statement: state the names and formulas of the common alkalis, limited to sodium hydroxide, $\ce{NaOH}$, potassium hydroxide, $\ce{KOH}$, ammonia, $\ce{NH3}$
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: sodium hydroxide, potassium hydroxide, ammonia solution, alkali

### 9701-7.2.3 — Brønsted-Lowry theory of acids and bases (conceptual)
Statement: describe the Brønsted-Lowry theory of acids and bases
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.1 Common acids: names and formulas; 9701-7.2.2 Common alkalis: names and formulas
Glossary: brønsted-lowry theory, proton donor, proton acceptor

### 9701-7.2.4 — Strong versus weak acids and bases (conceptual)
Statement: describe strong acids and strong bases as fully dissociated in aqueous solution and weak acids and weak bases as partially dissociated in aqueous solution
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.3 Brønsted-Lowry theory of acids and bases
Glossary: strong acid, weak acid, full dissociation, partial dissociation

### 9701-7.2.5 — The pH scale (factual)
Statement: appreciate that water has pH of 7, acid solutions pH of below 7 and alkaline solutions pH of above 7
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.3 Brønsted-Lowry theory of acids and bases
Glossary: ph scale, neutral ph, acidic ph, alkaline ph

### 9701-7.2.6 — Behavioural differences between strong and weak acids (conceptual)
Statement: explain qualitatively the differences in behaviour between strong and weak acids including the reaction with a reactive metal and difference in pH values by use of a pH meter, universal indicator or conductivity
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.4 Strong versus weak acids and bases; 9701-7.2.5 The pH scale
Glossary: strong acid, weak acid, reaction rate with metal, ph meter, conductivity

### 9701-7.2.7 — Neutralisation as H+ and OH- forming water (conceptual)
Statement: understand that neutralisation reactions occur when $\ce{H+ (aq)}$ and $\ce{OH- (aq)}$ form $\ce{H2O (l)}$
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.3 Brønsted-Lowry theory of acids and bases
Glossary: neutralisation, hydrogen ion, hydroxide ion, water formation

### 9701-7.2.8 — Salt formation in neutralisation (factual)
Statement: understand that salts are formed in neutralisation reactions
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.7 Neutralisation as H+ and OH- forming water
Glossary: salt formation, neutralisation reaction, acid-base reaction

### 9701-7.2.9 — Sketching pH titration curves (procedural)
Statement: sketch the pH titration curves of titrations using combinations of strong and weak acids with strong and weak alkalis
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.5 The pH scale; 9701-7.2.4 Strong versus weak acids and bases
Glossary: ph titration curve, equivalence point, strong acid-strong base curve, buffer region

### 9701-7.2.10 — Selecting titration indicators (procedural)
Statement: select suitable indicators for acid-alkali titrations, given appropriate data (pKa values will not be used)
Prerequisites: 9701-7.2.9 Sketching pH titration curves
Glossary: titration indicator, end point, colour change range
