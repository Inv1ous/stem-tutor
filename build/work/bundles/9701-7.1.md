## KCs

### 9701-7.1.1 — Reversible reactions and dynamic equilibrium (conceptual)
Statement: (a) understand what is meant by a reversible reaction (b) understand what is meant by dynamic equilibrium in terms of the rate of forward and reverse reactions being equal and the concentration of reactants and products remaining constant (c) understand the need for a closed system in order to establish dynamic equilibrium
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: reversible reaction, dynamic equilibrium, closed system, equal forward and reverse rates

### 9701-7.1.2 — Le Chatelier's principle (factual)
Statement: define Le Chatelier's principle as: if a change is made to a system at dynamic equilibrium, the position of equilibrium moves to minimise this change
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.1 Reversible reactions and dynamic equilibrium
Glossary: le chatelier's principle, position of equilibrium, minimise the change

### 9701-7.1.3 — Applying Le Chatelier's principle (procedural)
Statement: use Le Chatelier's principle to deduce qualitatively (from appropriate information) the effects of changes in temperature, concentration, pressure or presence of a catalyst on a system at equilibrium
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.2 Le Chatelier's principle
Glossary: le chatelier's principle, position of equilibrium, temperature change, pressure change

### 9701-7.1.4 — Equilibrium constant Kc expressions (procedural)
Statement: deduce expressions for equilibrium constants in terms of concentrations, $K_c$
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.1 Reversible reactions and dynamic equilibrium
Glossary: equilibrium constant, kc expression, concentration terms

### 9701-7.1.5 — Mole fraction and partial pressure (factual)
Statement: use the terms mole fraction and partial pressure
Prerequisites: 9701-2.2.1 The mole and the Avogadro constant
Glossary: mole fraction, partial pressure, total pressure

### 9701-7.1.6 — Equilibrium constant Kp expressions (procedural)
Statement: deduce expressions for equilibrium constants in terms of partial pressures, $K_p$ (use of the relationship between $K_p$ and $K_c$ is not required)
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.4 Equilibrium constant Kc expressions; 9701-7.1.5 Mole fraction and partial pressure
Glossary: equilibrium constant, kp expression, partial pressure

### 9701-7.1.7 — Calculations using Kc and Kp (procedural)
Statement: use the $K_c$ and $K_p$ expressions to carry out calculations (such calculations will not require the solving of quadratic equations)
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.4 Equilibrium constant Kc expressions; 9701-7.1.6 Equilibrium constant Kp expressions
Glossary: kc calculation, kp calculation, equilibrium constant

### 9701-7.1.8 — Calculating equilibrium quantities (procedural)
Statement: calculate the quantities present at equilibrium, given appropriate data
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.7 Calculations using Kc and Kp
Glossary: equilibrium quantities, ice table, equilibrium moles

### 9701-7.1.9 — Effect of conditions on the equilibrium constant (conceptual)
Statement: state whether changes in temperature, concentration or pressure or the presence of a catalyst affect the value of the equilibrium constant for a reaction
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.7 Calculations using Kc and Kp; 9701-7.1.3 Applying Le Chatelier's principle
Glossary: equilibrium constant value, temperature dependence, catalyst has no effect

### 9701-7.1.10 — Haber process and Contact process conditions (conceptual)
Statement: describe and explain the conditions used in the Haber process and the Contact process, as examples of the importance of an understanding of dynamic equilibrium in the chemical industry and the application of Le Chatelier's principle
Prerequisites: 9701-7.1.3 Applying Le Chatelier's principle; 9701-7.1.9 Effect of conditions on the equilibrium constant
Glossary: haber process, contact process, compromise conditions, le chatelier's principle
