## KCs

### 9701-9.2.1 — Period 3 reactions with oxygen, chlorine and water (procedural)
Statement: describe, and write equations for, the reactions of the elements with oxygen (to give $\ce{Na2O}$, $\ce{MgO}$, $\ce{Al2O3}$, $\ce{P4O10}$, $\ce{SO2}$), chlorine (to give $\ce{NaCl}$, $\ce{MgCl2}$, $\ce{AlCl3}$, $\ce{SiCl4}$, $\ce{PCl5}$) and water (Na and Mg only)
Prerequisites: 9701-3.2.1 Definition of ionic bonding; 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: period 3 oxides, period 3 chlorides, reaction with oxygen, reaction with water

### 9701-9.2.2 — Oxidation number trend in Period 3 compounds (conceptual)
Statement: state and explain the variation in the oxidation number of the oxides ($\ce{Na2O}$, $\ce{MgO}$, $\ce{Al2O3}$, $\ce{P4O10}$, $\ce{SO2}$ and $\ce{SO3}$ only) and chlorides ($\ce{NaCl}$, $\ce{MgCl2}$, $\ce{AlCl3}$, $\ce{SiCl4}$, $\ce{PCl5}$ only) in terms of their outer shell (valence shell) electrons
Prerequisites: 9701-9.2.1 Period 3 reactions with oxygen, chlorine and water; 9701-6.1.1 Calculating oxidation numbers
Glossary: oxidation number variation, valence shell electrons, period 3 oxides, period 3 chlorides

### 9701-9.2.3 — Reactions of Period 3 oxides with water (procedural)
Statement: describe, and write equations for, the reactions, if any, of the oxides $\ce{Na2O}$, $\ce{MgO}$, $\ce{Al2O3}$, $\ce{SiO2}$, $\ce{P4O10}$, $\ce{SO2}$ and $\ce{SO3}$ with water including the likely pHs of the solutions obtained
Prerequisites: 9701-9.2.1 Period 3 reactions with oxygen, chlorine and water
Glossary: oxide reactions with water, ph of oxide solutions, period 3 oxides

### 9701-9.2.4 — Acid-base behaviour of Period 3 oxides and hydroxides (conceptual)
Statement: describe, explain, and write equations for, the acid/base behaviour of the oxides $\ce{Na2O}$, $\ce{MgO}$, $\ce{Al2O3}$, $\ce{P4O10}$, $\ce{SO2}$ and $\ce{SO3}$ and the hydroxides $\ce{NaOH}$, $\ce{Mg(OH)2}$ and $\ce{Al(OH)3}$ including, where relevant, amphoteric behaviour in reactions with acids and bases (sodium hydroxide only)
Prerequisites: 9701-9.2.3 Reactions of Period 3 oxides with water; 9701-7.2.3 Brønsted-Lowry theory of acids and bases
Glossary: amphoteric oxide, amphoteric hydroxide, acid-base behaviour, period 3 oxides

### 9701-9.2.5 — Reactions of Period 3 chlorides with water (procedural)
Statement: describe, explain, and write equations for, the reactions of the chlorides $\ce{NaCl}$, $\ce{MgCl2}$, $\ce{AlCl3}$, $\ce{SiCl4}$, $\ce{PCl5}$ with water including the likely pHs of the solutions obtained
Prerequisites: 9701-9.2.1 Period 3 reactions with oxygen, chlorine and water
Glossary: chloride hydrolysis, ph of chloride solutions, period 3 chlorides

### 9701-9.2.6 — Explaining Period 3 oxide and chloride trends (conceptual)
Statement: explain the variations and trends in 9.2.2, 9.2.3, 9.2.4 and 9.2.5 in terms of bonding and electronegativity
Prerequisites: 9701-9.2.2 Oxidation number trend in Period 3 compounds; 9701-9.2.3 Reactions of Period 3 oxides with water; 9701-9.2.4 Acid-base behaviour of Period 3 oxides and hydroxides; 9701-9.2.5 Reactions of Period 3 chlorides with water
Glossary: bonding and electronegativity, period 3 trend, ionic to covalent character

### 9701-9.2.7 — Deducing bonding type from oxide and chloride properties (procedural)
Statement: suggest the types of chemical bonding present in the chlorides and oxides from observations of their chemical and physical properties
Prerequisites: 9701-9.2.6 Explaining Period 3 oxide and chloride trends
Glossary: bonding deduction, physical property evidence, period 3 chlorides and oxides
