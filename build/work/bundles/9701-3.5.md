## KCs

### 9701-3.5.1 — VSEPR shapes and bond angles (conceptual)
Statement: state and explain the shapes of, and bond angles in, molecules by using VSEPR theory, including as simple examples: $\ce{BF3}$ (trigonal planar, $120°$), $\ce{CO2}$ (linear, $180°$), $\ce{CH4}$ (tetrahedral, $109.5°$), $\ce{NH3}$ (pyramidal, $107°$), $\ce{H2O}$ (non-linear, $104.5°$), $\ce{SF6}$ (octahedral, $90°$), $\ce{PF5}$ (trigonal bipyramidal, $120°$ and $90°$)
Prerequisites: 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: vsepr theory, trigonal planar, tetrahedral, pyramidal, octahedral, lone pair repulsion

### 9701-3.5.2 — Predicting shapes of analogous molecules (procedural)
Statement: predict the shapes of, and bond angles in, molecules and ions analogous to those specified in 3.5.1
Prerequisites: 9701-3.5.1 VSEPR shapes and bond angles
Glossary: vsepr theory, molecular shape, bond angle prediction
