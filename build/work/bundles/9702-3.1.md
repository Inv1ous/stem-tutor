## KCs

### 9702-3.1.1 — Mass as resistance to motion (conceptual)
Statement: understand that mass is the property of an object that resists change in motion
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: mass, inertia, resistance to change in motion

### 9702-3.1.2 — Newton's second law, F = ma (procedural)
Statement: recall $F = ma$ and solve problems using it, understanding that acceleration and resultant force are always in the same direction
Prerequisites: 9702-3.1.1 Mass as resistance to motion; 9702-2.1.1 Defining distance, speed, velocity, acceleration
Glossary: newton's second law, f = ma, resultant force

### 9702-3.1.3 — Defining linear momentum (procedural)
Statement: define and use linear momentum as the product of mass and velocity
Prerequisites: 9702-2.1.1 Defining distance, speed, velocity, acceleration
Glossary: linear momentum, p = mv, momentum

### 9702-3.1.4 — Force as rate of change of momentum (procedural)
Statement: define and use force as rate of change of momentum
Prerequisites: 9702-3.1.2 Newton's second law, F = ma; 9702-3.1.3 Defining linear momentum
Glossary: rate of change of momentum, newton's second law, impulse

### 9702-3.1.5 — Newton's three laws of motion (procedural)
Statement: state and apply each of Newton's laws of motion
Prerequisites: 9702-3.1.1 Mass as resistance to motion; 9702-3.1.2 Newton's second law, F = ma; 9702-3.1.4 Force as rate of change of momentum
Glossary: newton's first law, newton's second law, newton's third law, laws of motion

### 9702-3.1.6 — Weight as a gravitational force (procedural)
Statement: describe and use the concept of weight as the effect of a gravitational field on a mass and recall that the weight of an object is equal to the product of its mass and the acceleration of free fall
Prerequisites: 9702-3.1.2 Newton's second law, F = ma
Glossary: weight, gravitational field, w = mg, acceleration of free fall
