Spec: CAIE AS & A Level Chemistry 9701 (2025-2027). Pack file: `build/out/packs/9701/9701-1.3.json`. Note path: `Subjects/9701 Chemistry/01 Atomic structure/1.3 Electrons, energy levels and atomic orbitals.md` (write it under `build/out/notes/`). Diagram files: `Assets/9701/9701-1.3-<name>.svg`.

## KCs

### 9701-1.3.1 — Shells, sub-shells, orbitals and quantum number (factual)
Statement: understand the terms: (a) shells, sub-shells and orbitals (b) principal quantum number ($n$) (c) ground state, limited to electronic configuration
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.1 Atomic structure: nucleus and electron shells
Glossary: shell, sub-shell, orbital, principal quantum number, ground state

### 9701-1.3.2 — Orbitals and electron capacity of s, p, d (factual)
Statement: describe the number of orbitals making up s, p and d sub-shells, and the number of electrons that can fill s, p and d sub-shells
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.1 Shells, sub-shells, orbitals and quantum number
Glossary: s sub-shell, p sub-shell, d sub-shell, orbital capacity, two electrons per orbital

### 9701-1.3.3 — Energy order of sub-shells (factual)
Statement: describe the order of increasing energy of the sub-shells within the first three shells and the 4s and 4p sub-shells
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.2 Orbitals and electron capacity of s, p, d
Glossary: sub-shell energy order, 4s before 3d, aufbau order

### 9701-1.3.4 — Writing electronic configurations (procedural)
Statement: describe the electronic configurations to include the number of electrons in each shell, sub-shell and orbital
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.3 Energy order of sub-shells
Glossary: electronic configuration, sub-shell notation, orbital diagram

### 9701-1.3.5 — Electron configuration and inter-electron repulsion (conceptual)
Statement: explain the electronic configurations in terms of energy of the electrons and inter-electron repulsion
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.4 Writing electronic configurations
Glossary: inter-electron repulsion, electron energy, hund's rule, electronic configuration

### 9701-1.3.6 — Deducing electronic configuration of atoms and ions (procedural)
Statement: determine the electronic configuration of atoms and ions given the atomic or proton number and charge, using either of the following conventions: e.g. for Fe: $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^6 3d^6 4s^2$ (full electronic configuration) or $\ce{[Ar]}\,3d^6 4s^2$ (shorthand electronic configuration)
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.4 Writing electronic configurations; 9701-1.1.6 Counting protons, neutrons and electrons
Glossary: full electronic configuration, shorthand configuration, noble gas core, ion configuration

### 9701-1.3.7 — Electrons-in-boxes notation (procedural)
Statement: understand and use the electrons in boxes notation, e.g. for Fe: $\ce{[Ar]}$ 3d and 4s sub-shells shown as boxes with arrows representing paired and unpaired electrons
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.6 Deducing electronic configuration of atoms and ions
Glossary: electrons in boxes, orbital diagram, paired electrons, unpaired electrons

### 9701-1.3.8 — Shapes of s and p orbitals (factual)
Statement: describe and sketch the shapes of s and p orbitals
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.1 Shells, sub-shells, orbitals and quantum number
Glossary: s orbital shape, p orbital shape, spherical, dumbbell

### 9701-1.3.9 — Definition of a free radical (factual)
Statement: describe a free radical as a species with one or more unpaired electrons
Prerequisites: 9701-1.3.2 Orbitals and electron capacity of s, p, d
Glossary: free radical, unpaired electron, radical species
