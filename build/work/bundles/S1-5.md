## KCs

### S1-5.1 — Discrete random variable (conceptual)
Statement: The concept of a discrete random variable.
Prerequisites: S1-3.1 Elementary probability
Glossary: discrete random variable, random variable, probability distribution

### S1-5.2 — Probability and cumulative distribution functions (procedural)
Statement: The probability function and the cumulative distribution function for a discrete random variable.
Guidance: Simple uses of the probability function $p(x)$ where $p(x) = P(X = x)$. Use of the cumulative distribution function $F(x_0) = P(X \le x_0) = \sum_{x \le x_0} p(x)$.
Prerequisites: S1-5.1 Discrete random variable
Glossary: probability function, p(x) = p(x = x), cumulative distribution function, f(x0) = p(x ≤ x0)

### S1-5.3 — Mean and variance of a discrete RV (procedural)
Statement: Mean and variance of a discrete random variable.
Guidance: Use of $E(X)$, $E(X^2)$ for calculating the variance of $X$. Knowledge and use of $E(aX+b) = aE(X)+b$, $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b) = a^2\operatorname{Var}(X)$.
Prerequisites: S1-5.2 Probability and cumulative distribution functions
Glossary: e(x), var(x), e(ax+b) = ae(x)+b, var(ax+b) = a^2var(x)

### S1-5.4 — Discrete uniform distribution (procedural)
Statement: The discrete uniform distribution.
Guidance: The mean and variance of this distribution.
Prerequisites: S1-5.3 Mean and variance of a discrete RV
Glossary: discrete uniform distribution, mean of discrete uniform distribution, variance of discrete uniform distribution
