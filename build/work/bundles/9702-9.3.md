## KCs

### 9702-9.3.1 — Defining resistance (factual)
Statement: define resistance
Prerequisites: 9702-9.2.2 Using V = W/Q
Glossary: resistance, ohm, opposition to current

### 9702-9.3.2 — Using V = IR (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $V = IR$
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.1 Defining resistance
Glossary: resistance, v = ir, ohm's law equation

### 9702-9.3.3 — Sketching I-V characteristics (procedural)
Statement: sketch the I–V characteristics of a metallic conductor at constant temperature, a semiconductor diode and a filament lamp
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.2 Using V = IR
Glossary: i-v characteristic, semiconductor diode, filament lamp

### 9702-9.3.4 — Filament lamp resistance and temperature (conceptual)
Statement: explain that the resistance of a filament lamp increases as current increases because its temperature increases
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.3 Sketching I-V characteristics
Glossary: filament lamp resistance, temperature and resistance, non-ohmic behaviour

### 9702-9.3.5 — Ohm's law (factual)
Statement: state Ohm's law
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.2 Using V = IR
Glossary: ohm's law, constant temperature, proportional current and voltage

### 9702-9.3.6 — Resistivity, R = ρL/A (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $R = \rho L/A$
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.1 Defining resistance
Glossary: resistivity, r = rho l / a, resistivity formula

### 9702-9.3.7 — Light-dependent resistor behaviour (conceptual)
Statement: understand that the resistance of a light-dependent resistor (LDR) decreases as the light intensity increases
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.1 Defining resistance
Glossary: light-dependent resistor, ldr, resistance and light intensity

### 9702-9.3.8 — Thermistor resistance and temperature (conceptual)
Statement: understand that the resistance of a thermistor decreases as the temperature increases (it will be assumed that thermistors have a negative temperature coefficient)
Prerequisites: 9702-9.3.1 Defining resistance
Glossary: thermistor, negative temperature coefficient, resistance and temperature
