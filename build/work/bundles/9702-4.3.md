### 9702-4.3.3 — Deriving hydrostatic pressure (procedural)
Statement: derive, from the definitions of pressure and density, the equation for hydrostatic pressure $\Delta p = \rho g \Delta h$
Prerequisites: 9702-4.3.1 Defining density; 9702-4.3.2 Defining pressure

Glossary: hydrostatic pressure, fluid depth, pressure difference

### 9702-4.3.5 — Upthrust from pressure difference (conceptual)
Statement: understand that the upthrust acting on an object in a fluid is due to a difference in hydrostatic pressure
Prerequisites: 9702-4.3.4 Using hydrostatic pressure equation
Glossary: upthrust, hydrostatic pressure difference, buoyancy

Statement: calculate the upthrust acting on an object in a fluid using the equation $F = \rho g V$ (Archimedes' principle)
Prerequisites: 9702-4.3.5 Upthrust from pressure difference
