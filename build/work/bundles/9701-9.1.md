### 9701-9.1.1 — Periodicity of physical properties across Period 3 (factual)
Statement: describe qualitatively (and indicate the periodicity in) the variations in atomic radius, ionic radius, melting point and electrical conductivity of the elements

Statement: explain the variation in melting point and electrical conductivity in terms of the structure and bonding of the elements
Prerequisites: 9701-9.1.1 Periodicity of physical properties across Period 3; 9701-4.2.1 Lattice structures of crystalline solids
Glossary: melting point trend, electrical conductivity trend, structure and bonding
