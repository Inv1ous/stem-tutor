## KCs

### P2-2.1 — Factor and remainder theorems (procedural)
Statement: Simple algebraic division; use of the Factor Theorem and the Remainder Theorem.
Guidance: Only division by $(ax+b)$ or $(ax-b)$ will be required, e.g. students should know that if $f(x)=0$ when $x=\dfrac{b}{a}$, then $(ax-b)$ is a factor of $f(x)$. Students may be required to factorise cubic expressions such as $x^3+3x^2-4$ and $6x^3+11x^2-x-6$. Students should be familiar with the terms 'quotient' and 'remainder' and be able to determine the remainder when the polynomial $f(x)$ is divided by $(ax+b)$.
Prerequisites: P1-1.10 Manipulating and factorising polynomials
Glossary: factor theorem, remainder theorem, algebraic division, quotient, remainder, factorising cubics
