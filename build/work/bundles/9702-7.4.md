## KCs

### 9702-7.4.1 — Electromagnetic waves travel at speed c (factual)
Statement: state that all electromagnetic waves are transverse waves that travel with the same speed c in free space
Prerequisites: 9702-7.2.1 Transverse versus longitudinal waves
Glossary: electromagnetic wave, transverse wave, speed of light c

### 9702-7.4.2 — Electromagnetic spectrum wavelength ranges (factual)
Statement: recall the approximate range of wavelengths in free space of the principal regions of the electromagnetic spectrum from radio waves to γ-rays
Prerequisites: 9702-7.4.1 Electromagnetic waves travel at speed c
Glossary: electromagnetic spectrum, radio waves, gamma rays, wavelength ranges

### 9702-7.4.3 — Visible light wavelength range (factual)
Statement: recall that wavelengths in the range 400–700 nm in free space are visible to the human eye
Prerequisites: 9702-7.4.2 Electromagnetic spectrum wavelength ranges
Glossary: visible light, 400-700 nm, visible spectrum
