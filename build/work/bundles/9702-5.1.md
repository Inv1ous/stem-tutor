## KCs

### 9702-5.1.1 — Work done by a force (procedural)
Statement: understand the concept of work, and recall and use work done = force × displacement in the direction of the force
Prerequisites: 9702-1.4.3 Resolving a vector into components
Glossary: work done, force x displacement, joule

### 9702-5.1.2 — Conservation of energy (procedural)
Statement: recall and apply the principle of conservation of energy
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.1 Work done by a force
Glossary: conservation of energy, energy transfer, total energy

### 9702-5.1.3 — Efficiency as a ratio of energies (conceptual)
Statement: recall and understand that the efficiency of a system is the ratio of useful energy output from the system to the total energy input
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.2 Conservation of energy
Glossary: efficiency, useful energy output, total energy input

### 9702-5.1.4 — Solving efficiency problems (procedural)
Statement: use the concept of efficiency to solve problems
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.3 Efficiency as a ratio of energies
Glossary: efficiency, energy output, energy input

### 9702-5.1.5 — Defining power (factual)
Statement: define power as work done per unit time
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.1 Work done by a force
Glossary: power, work done per unit time, watt

### 9702-5.1.6 — Solving power problems, P = W/t (procedural)
Statement: solve problems using $P = W/t$
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.5 Defining power
Glossary: power, p = w/t, work done per unit time

### 9702-5.1.7 — Deriving and using P = Fv (procedural)
Statement: derive $P = Fv$ and use it to solve problems
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.6 Solving power problems, P = W/t; 9702-2.1.1 Defining distance, speed, velocity, acceleration
