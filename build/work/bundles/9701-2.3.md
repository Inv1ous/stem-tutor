## KCs

### 9701-2.3.1 — Writing formulas of ionic compounds (procedural)
Statement: write formulas of ionic compounds from ionic charges and oxidation numbers (shown by a Roman numeral), including: (a) the prediction of ionic charge from the position of an element in the Periodic Table (b) recall of the names and formulas for the following ions: $\ce{NO3-}$, $\ce{CO3^2-}$, $\ce{SO4^2-}$, $\ce{OH-}$, $\ce{NH4+}$, $\ce{Zn^2+}$, $\ce{Ag+}$, $\ce{HCO3-}$, $\ce{PO4^3-}$
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: ionic formula, oxidation number, polyatomic ion, roman numeral notation

### 9701-2.3.2 — Writing and balancing chemical equations (procedural)
Statement: (a) write and construct equations (which should be balanced), including ionic equations (which should not include spectator ions) (b) use appropriate state symbols in equations
Prerequisites: 9701-2.3.1 Writing formulas of ionic compounds
Glossary: balanced equation, ionic equation, spectator ion, state symbols

### 9701-2.3.3 — Empirical and molecular formula definitions (factual)
Statement: define and use the terms empirical and molecular formula
Prerequisites: 9701-2.1.2 Relative atomic, isotopic, molecular and formula mass
Glossary: empirical formula, molecular formula, simplest ratio

### 9701-2.3.4 — Anhydrous, hydrated and water of crystallisation (factual)
Statement: understand and use the terms anhydrous, hydrated and water of crystallisation
Prerequisites: 9701-2.3.3 Empirical and molecular formula definitions
Glossary: anhydrous, hydrated, water of crystallisation

### 9701-2.3.5 — Calculating empirical and molecular formulas (procedural)
Statement: calculate empirical and molecular formulas, using given data
Prerequisites: 9701-2.3.3 Empirical and molecular formula definitions; 9701-2.2.1 The mole and the Avogadro constant
Glossary: empirical formula calculation, molecular formula calculation, mole ratio
