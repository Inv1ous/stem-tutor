## KCs

### 9701-8.2.1 — Definition of activation energy (factual)
Statement: define activation energy, $E_A$, as the minimum energy required for a collision to be effective
Prerequisites: 9701-8.1.1 Rate of reaction and effective collisions
Glossary: activation energy, minimum energy, effective collision

### 9701-8.2.2 — The Boltzmann distribution (procedural)
Statement: sketch and use the Boltzmann distribution to explain the significance of activation energy
Prerequisites: 9701-8.2.1 Definition of activation energy
Glossary: boltzmann distribution, activation energy, distribution of molecular energies

### 9701-8.2.3 — Effect of temperature on reaction rate (conceptual)
Statement: explain qualitatively, in terms both of the Boltzmann distribution and of frequency of effective collisions, the effect of temperature change on the rate of a reaction
Prerequisites: 9701-8.2.2 The Boltzmann distribution; 9701-8.1.2 Effect of concentration and pressure on rate
Glossary: temperature effect on rate, boltzmann distribution, frequency of effective collisions
