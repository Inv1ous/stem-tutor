## KCs

### 9701-3.4.1 — Covalent and dative covalent bonding (procedural)
Statement: define covalent bonding as electrostatic attraction between the nuclei of two atoms and a shared pair of electrons (a) describe covalent bonding in molecules including: hydrogen $\ce{H2}$, oxygen $\ce{O2}$, nitrogen $\ce{N2}$, chlorine $\ce{Cl2}$, hydrogen chloride $\ce{HCl}$, carbon dioxide $\ce{CO2}$, ammonia $\ce{NH3}$, methane $\ce{CH4}$, ethane $\ce{C2H6}$, ethene $\ce{C2H4}$ (b) understand that elements in period 3 can expand their octet including in the compounds sulfur dioxide $\ce{SO2}$, phosphorus pentachloride $\ce{PCl5}$, and sulfur hexafluoride $\ce{SF6}$ (c) describe coordinate (dative covalent) bonding, including in the reaction between ammonia and hydrogen chloride gases to form the ammonium ion, $\ce{NH4+}$, and in the $\ce{Al2Cl6}$ molecule
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.1 Definition of electronegativity
Glossary: covalent bond, shared pair of electrons, dative covalent bond, expanded octet, coordinate bond

### 9701-3.4.2 — Sigma and pi bonds, hybridisation (conceptual)
Statement: (a) describe covalent bonds in terms of orbital overlap giving $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds: $\sigma$ bonds are formed by direct overlap of orbitals between the bonding atoms; $\pi$ bonds are formed by the sideways overlap of adjacent p orbitals above and below the $\sigma$ bond (b) describe how the $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds form in molecules including $\ce{H2}$, $\ce{C2H6}$, $\ce{C2H4}$, $\ce{HCN}$ and $\ce{N2}$ (c) use the concept of hybridisation to describe sp, $sp^2$ and $sp^3$ orbitals
Prerequisites: 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: sigma bond, pi bond, orbital overlap, hybridisation, sp2 orbital

### 9701-3.4.3 — Bond energy and bond length (conceptual)
Statement: (a) define the terms: bond energy as the energy required to break one mole of a particular covalent bond in the gaseous state; bond length as the internuclear distance of two covalently bonded atoms (b) use bond energy values and the concept of bond length to compare the reactivity of covalent molecules
Prerequisites: 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: bond energy, bond length, internuclear distance, bond reactivity
