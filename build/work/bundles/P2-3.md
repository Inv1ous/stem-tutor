## KCs

### P2-3.1 — Equation and properties of a circle (procedural)
Statement: Coordinate geometry of the circle using the equation of a circle in the form $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$ and including use of the following circle properties: (i) the angle in a semicircle is a right angle; (ii) the perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord; (iii) the perpendicularity of radius and tangent.
Guidance: Students should be able to find the radius and the coordinates of the centre of the circle, given the equation of the circle and vice versa.
Prerequisites: P1-2.2 Parallel and perpendicular lines
Glossary: equation of a circle, (x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2, angle in a semicircle, radius and tangent, perpendicular bisector of a chord
