## KCs

### P1-4.1 — Derivative as gradient and rate of change (conceptual)
Statement: The derivative of $f(x)$ as the gradient of the tangent to the graph of $y=f(x)$ at a point; the gradient of the tangent as a limit; interpretation as a rate of change; second order derivatives.
Guidance: For example, knowledge that $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ is the rate of change of $y$ with respect to $x$. Knowledge of the chain rule is not required. The notation $f'(x)$ and $f''(x)$ may be used.
Prerequisites: P1-1.11 Sketching curves and using intersections
Glossary: derivative, gradient of a tangent, rate of change, dy/dx, second derivative, f'(x)

### P1-4.2 — Differentiating powers of x (procedural)
Statement: Differentiation of $x^n$, and related sums, differences and constant multiples.
Guidance: The ability to differentiate expressions such as $(2x+5)(x-1)$ and $\dfrac{2x^2+5x-3}{3x}$ is expected.
Prerequisites: P1-4.1 Derivative as gradient and rate of change; P1-1.10 Manipulating and factorising polynomials
Glossary: differentiating x^n, power rule, sums and differences, constant multiple rule, d/dx(x^n) = nx^{n-1}

### P1-4.3 — Tangents and normals (procedural)
Statement: Applications of differentiation to gradients, tangents and normals.
Guidance: Use of differentiation to find equations of tangents and normals at specific points on a curve.
Prerequisites: P1-4.2 Differentiating powers of x; P1-2.1 Equations of straight lines
Glossary: tangent to a curve, normal to a curve, gradient at a point, equation of a tangent, equation of a normal
