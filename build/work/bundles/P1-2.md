## KCs

### P1-2.1 — Equations of straight lines (procedural)
Statement: Equation of a straight line, including the forms $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ and $ax+by+c=0$.
Guidance: To include: (i) the equation of a line through two given points; (ii) the equation of a line parallel (or perpendicular) to a given line through a given point. For example, the line perpendicular to the line $3x+4y=18$ through the point $(2,3)$ has equation $y-3=\dfrac{4}{3}(x-2)$.
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: equation of a straight line, gradient, y - y1 = m(x - x1), ax + by + c = 0, line through two points

### P1-2.2 — Parallel and perpendicular lines (conceptual)
Statement: Conditions for two straight lines to be parallel or perpendicular to each other.
Prerequisites: P1-2.1 Equations of straight lines
Glossary: parallel lines, perpendicular lines, gradient condition, m1 = m2, m1 m2 = -1
