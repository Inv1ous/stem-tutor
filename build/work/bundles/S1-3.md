## KCs

### S1-3.1 — Elementary probability (factual)
Statement: Elementary probability.
Prerequisites: S1-1.1 Modelling in probability and statistics
Glossary: probability, equally likely outcomes, probability scale, event

### S1-3.2 — Conditional and complementary probability (procedural)
Statement: Sample space. Exclusive and complementary events. Conditional probability.
Guidance: Understanding and use of $P(A') = 1 - P(A)$, $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$, $P(A \cap B) = P(A)\,P(B \mid A)$.
Prerequisites: S1-3.1 Elementary probability
Glossary: sample space, mutually exclusive events, complementary event, conditional probability, p(a|b)

### S1-3.3 — Independent events (procedural)
Statement: Independence of two events.
Guidance: $P(B \mid A) = P(B)$, $P(A \mid B) = P(A)$, $P(A \cap B) = P(A)\,P(B)$.
Prerequisites: S1-3.2 Conditional and complementary probability
Glossary: independent events, p(b|a) = p(b), p(a ∩ b) = p(a)p(b)

### S1-3.4 — Tree diagrams and Venn diagrams (procedural)
Statement: Sum and product laws.
Guidance: Use of tree diagrams and Venn diagrams. Sampling with and without replacement.
Prerequisites: S1-3.2 Conditional and complementary probability; S1-3.3 Independent events
Glossary: sum law, product law, tree diagram, venn diagram, sampling with replacement, sampling without replacement

- **Explain**: Give reasons in context.
