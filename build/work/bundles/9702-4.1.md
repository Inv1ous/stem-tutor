## KCs

### 9702-4.1.1 — Centre of gravity (conceptual)
Statement: understand that the weight of an object may be taken as acting at a single point known as its centre of gravity

Statement: define and apply the moment of a force
Prerequisites: 9702-4.1.1 Centre of gravity
Glossary: moment of a force, turning effect, moment = force x perpendicular distance

### 9702-4.1.3 — Couples as pure rotation forces (conceptual)

Prerequisites: 9702-4.1.2 Moment of a force
