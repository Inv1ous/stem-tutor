## KCs

### 9702-3.2.1 — Friction and drag forces (conceptual)
Statement: show a qualitative understanding of frictional forces and viscous/drag forces including air resistance (no treatment of the coefficients of friction and viscosity is required, and a simple model of drag force increasing as speed increases is sufficient)
Prerequisites: 9702-3.1.5 Newton's three laws of motion
Glossary: friction, viscous force, drag force, air resistance

### 9702-3.2.2 — Falling motion with air resistance (conceptual)
Statement: describe and explain qualitatively the motion of objects in a uniform gravitational field with air resistance
Prerequisites: 9702-3.2.1 Friction and drag forces; 9702-2.1.7 Solving uniformly accelerated motion problems
Glossary: air resistance, terminal velocity, projectile with drag

### 9702-3.2.3 — Reaching terminal velocity (conceptual)
Statement: understand that objects moving against a resistive force may reach a terminal (constant) velocity
Prerequisites: 9702-3.2.2 Falling motion with air resistance
Glossary: terminal velocity, resistive force, drag
