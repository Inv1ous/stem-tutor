## KCs

### 9701-3.6.1 — Hydrogen bonding and anomalous water properties (conceptual)
Statement: (a) describe hydrogen bonding, limited to molecules containing N-H and O-H groups, including ammonia and water as simple examples (b) use the concept of hydrogen bonding to explain the anomalous properties of $\ce{H2O}$ (ice and water): its relatively high melting and boiling points; its relatively high surface tension; the density of the solid ice compared with the liquid water
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.3 Trends in electronegativity; 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: hydrogen bonding, anomalous properties of water, surface tension, ice density

### 9701-3.6.2 — Electronegativity, bond polarity and dipole moments (conceptual)
Statement: use the concept of electronegativity to explain bond polarity and dipole moments of molecules
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.3 Trends in electronegativity; 9701-3.4.1 Covalent and dative covalent bonding
Glossary: bond polarity, dipole moment, electronegativity difference

### 9701-3.6.3 — Van der Waals' forces: id-id and pd-pd (conceptual)
Statement: (a) describe van der Waals' forces as the intermolecular forces between molecular entities other than those due to bond formation, and use the term van der Waals' forces as a generic term to describe all intermolecular forces (b) describe the types of van der Waals' forces: instantaneous dipole-induced dipole (id-id) forces, also called London dispersion forces; permanent dipole-permanent dipole (pd-pd) forces, including hydrogen bonding (c) describe hydrogen bonding and understand that hydrogen bonding is a special case of permanent dipole-permanent dipole forces between molecules where hydrogen is bonded to a highly electronegative atom
Prerequisites: 9701-3.6.2 Electronegativity, bond polarity and dipole moments
Glossary: van der waals forces, instantaneous dipole-induced dipole, london dispersion forces, permanent dipole-permanent dipole, hydrogen bonding

### 9701-3.6.4 — Relative strength of bonding versus intermolecular forces (factual)
Statement: state that, in general, ionic, covalent and metallic bonding are stronger than intermolecular forces
Prerequisites: 9701-3.6.3 Van der Waals' forces: id-id and pd-pd; 9701-3.2.1 Definition of ionic bonding
Glossary: bond strength, intermolecular forces, ionic bonding, covalent bonding
