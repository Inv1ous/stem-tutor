## KCs

### P2-6.1 — Trigonometric identities tan and Pythagorean (conceptual)
Statement: Knowledge and use of $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ and $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$.
Prerequisites: P1-3.3 Trig function graphs and periodicity
Glossary: tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, sin^2 θ + cos^2 θ = 1, pythagorean identity, trigonometric identity

### P2-6.2 — Solving trigonometric equations (procedural)
Statement: Solution of simple trigonometric equations in a given interval.
Guidance: Students should be able to solve equations such as $\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\dfrac{3}{4}$ for $0<x<2\pi$, $\cos(x+30^{\circ})=\dfrac{1}{2}$ for $-180^{\circ}<x<180^{\circ}$, $\tan 2x=1$ for $90^{\circ}<x<270^{\circ}$, $6\cos^2 x+\sin x-5=0$ for $0 \leq x<360^{\circ}$, $\sin\left(2x+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}$ for $-\pi \leq x<\pi$.
Prerequisites: P2-6.1 Trigonometric identities tan and Pythagorean; P1-3.3 Trig function graphs and periodicity
Glossary: solving trig equations, given interval, principal values, trigonometric equation, multiple solutions in an interval
