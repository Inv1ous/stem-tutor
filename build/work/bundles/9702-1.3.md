## KCs

### 9702-1.3.1 — Systematic and random errors (conceptual)
Statement: understand and explain the effects of systematic errors (including zero errors) and random errors in measurements
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: systematic error, random error, zero error, measurement error

### 9702-1.3.2 — Precision versus accuracy (conceptual)
Statement: understand the distinction between precision and accuracy
Prerequisites: 9702-1.3.1 Systematic and random errors
Glossary: precision, accuracy, measurement error

### 9702-1.3.3 — Combining uncertainties in derived quantities (procedural)
Statement: assess the uncertainty in a derived quantity by simple addition of absolute or percentage uncertainties
Prerequisites: 9702-1.3.1 Systematic and random errors; 9702-1.3.2 Precision versus accuracy
Glossary: percentage uncertainty, absolute uncertainty, combining uncertainties, error propagation
