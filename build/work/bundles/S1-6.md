### S1-6.1 — The Normal distribution (procedural)
Statement: The Normal distribution including the mean, variance and use of tables of the cumulative distribution function.
Guidance: Knowledge of the shape and the symmetry of the distribution is required. Knowledge of the probability density function is not required. Derivation of the mean, variance and cumulative distribution function is not required. Interpolation is not necessary. Questions may involve the solution of simultaneous equations.
Prerequisites: S1-5.3 Mean and variance of a discrete RV; S1-2.3 Variance, standard deviation, IQR
Glossary: normal distribution, standard normal tables, symmetry, probability density function, simultaneous equations
