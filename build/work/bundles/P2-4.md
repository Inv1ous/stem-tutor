## KCs

### P2-4.1 — Sequences and recurrence relations (procedural)
Statement: Sequences, including those given by a formula for the $n$th term and those generated by a simple relation of the form $x_{n+1}=f(x_n)$.
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: nth term formula, recurrence relation, x_{n+1} = f(x_n), sequence, term-to-term rule

### P2-4.2 — Arithmetic sequences and series (procedural)
Statement: Understand and work with arithmetic sequences and series, including the formula for the nth term and the sum of a finite arithmetic series; the sum of the first n natural numbers.
Guidance: The proof of the sum formula should be known. Understanding of $\Sigma$ notation will be expected.
Prerequisites: P2-4.1 Sequences and recurrence relations
Glossary: arithmetic sequence, arithmetic series, nth term, sum of an arithmetic series, sigma notation

### P2-4.3 — Increasing, decreasing and periodic sequences (factual)
Statement: Increasing sequences, decreasing sequences and periodic sequences.
Prerequisites: P2-4.1 Sequences and recurrence relations
Glossary: increasing sequence, decreasing sequence, periodic sequence, sequence behaviour

### P2-4.4 — Geometric sequences and series (procedural)
Statement: Understand and work with geometric sequences and series, including the formulae for the $n$th term and the sum of a finite geometric series; the sum to infinity of a convergent geometric series, including the use of $|r|<1$. Binomial expansion of $(a+bx)^n$ for
Guidance: For example, given the sum of a series students should be able to use logarithms to find the value of $n$. The proof of the sum formula for a finite series should be known. The sum to infinity may be expressed as $S_\infty$.
Prerequisites: P2-4.1 Sequences and recurrence relations
Glossary: geometric sequence, geometric series, sum to infinity, |r| < 1, common ratio

### P2-4.5 — Binomial expansion for positive integer n (procedural)
Statement: positive integer $n$.
Guidance: The notations $n!$, $\dbinom{n}{r}$ and ${}^nC_r$ may be used.
Prerequisites: P2-4.4 Geometric sequences and series
Glossary: binomial expansion, n choose r, nCr, factorial notation, (a+bx)^n

## Command words

- **Show that**: Prove a given result; every step must be shown (answer given).
- **Hence**: Use the previous result to reach the answer.
- **Hence or otherwise**: Using the previous result is expected, but any valid method earns the marks.
- **Find**: Work out; method marks need working.
- **Calculate**: Work out from given facts; show working.
- **Prove**: Give a complete logical argument.
- **Sketch**: Draw the shape with key features labelled (intercepts, asymptotes, turning points), not to scale.
- **Exact**: Give the answer in surd, fraction, pi or ln form, not a decimal.
- **Verify**: Show that given values satisfy the statement.
- **State**: Give the answer without working.
- **Explain**: Give reasons in context.
- **Interpret**: Explain the meaning of a result in the context of the question.
