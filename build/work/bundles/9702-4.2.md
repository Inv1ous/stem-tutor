### 9702-4.2.2 — Equilibrium of forces and torques (conceptual)
Statement: understand that, when there is no resultant force and no resultant torque, a system is in equilibrium
Prerequisites: 9702-4.2.1 Principle of moments; 9702-4.1.4 Torque of a couple
Glossary: equilibrium, resultant force, resultant torque
