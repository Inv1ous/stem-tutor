## KCs

### P1-3.1 — Sine rule, cosine rule and triangle area (procedural)
Statement: The sine and cosine rules, and the area of a triangle in the form $\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$.
Guidance: Including the ambiguous case of the sine rule.
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: sine rule, cosine rule, ambiguous case, area of a triangle, 1/2 ab sin C

### P1-3.2 — Radians, arc length and sector area (procedural)
Statement: Radian measure, including use for arc length and area of sector.
Guidance: Use of the formulae $s=r\theta$ and $A=\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$.
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: radian measure, arc length, s = rθ, area of a sector, A = 1/2 r^2 θ

### P1-3.3 — Trig function graphs and periodicity (procedural)
Statement: Sine, cosine and tangent functions. Their graphs, symmetries and periodicity.
Guidance: Knowledge of graphs of curves with equations such as $y=3\sin x$, $y=\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$, $y=\sin 2x$ is expected.
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: periodicity, amplitude, sine graph, cosine graph, tangent graph, symmetry of trig graphs
