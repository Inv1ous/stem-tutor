## KCs

### 9701-2.1.1 — Definition of the unified atomic mass unit (factual)
Statement: define the unified atomic mass unit as one twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: unified atomic mass unit, carbon-12, one twelfth

### 9701-2.1.2 — Relative atomic, isotopic, molecular and formula mass (factual)
Statement: define relative atomic mass, $A_r$, relative isotopic mass, relative molecular mass, $M_r$, and relative formula mass in terms of the unified atomic mass unit
Prerequisites: 9701-2.1.1 Definition of the unified atomic mass unit
Glossary: relative atomic mass, relative isotopic mass, relative molecular mass, relative formula mass
