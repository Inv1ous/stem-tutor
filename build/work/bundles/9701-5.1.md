## KCs

### 9701-5.1.1 — Exothermic and endothermic reactions (factual)
Statement: understand that chemical reactions are accompanied by enthalpy changes and these changes can be exothermic ($\Delta H$ is negative) or endothermic ($\Delta H$ is positive)
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: exothermic, endothermic, enthalpy change, delta h

### 9701-5.1.2 — Reaction pathway diagrams (procedural)
Statement: construct and interpret a reaction pathway diagram, in terms of the enthalpy change of the reaction and of the activation energy
Prerequisites: 9701-5.1.1 Exothermic and endothermic reactions
Glossary: reaction pathway diagram, enthalpy change, activation energy

### 9701-5.1.3 — Standard conditions and enthalpy change terms (factual)
Statement: define and use the terms: (a) standard conditions (this syllabus assumes that these are $298\text{ K}$ and $101\text{ kPa}$) shown by $\ominus$ (b) enthalpy change with particular reference to: reaction, $\Delta H_r$, formation, $\Delta H_f$, combustion, $\Delta H_c$, neutralisation, $\Delta H_{neut}$
Prerequisites: 9701-5.1.1 Exothermic and endothermic reactions
Glossary: standard conditions, enthalpy of formation, enthalpy of combustion, enthalpy of neutralisation, standard enthalpy of reaction

### 9701-5.1.4 — Energy transfer from bond breaking and making (conceptual)
Statement: understand that energy transfers occur during chemical reactions because of the breaking and making of chemical bonds
Prerequisites: 9701-5.1.1 Exothermic and endothermic reactions; 9701-3.4.3 Bond energy and bond length
Glossary: bond breaking, bond making, energy transfer

### 9701-5.1.5 — Calculating enthalpy change from bond energies (procedural)
Statement: use bond energies ($\Delta H$ positive, i.e. bond breaking) to calculate enthalpy change of reaction, $\Delta H_r$
Prerequisites: 9701-5.1.4 Energy transfer from bond breaking and making; 9701-3.4.3 Bond energy and bond length
Glossary: bond energy calculation, enthalpy of reaction, bonds broken minus bonds formed

### 9701-5.1.6 — Exact versus average bond energies (conceptual)
Statement: understand that some bond energies are exact and some bond energies are averages
Prerequisites: 9701-5.1.5 Calculating enthalpy change from bond energies
Glossary: exact bond energy, average bond energy, bond energy data

### 9701-5.1.7 — Calculating enthalpy change from experimental data (procedural)
Statement: calculate enthalpy changes from appropriate experimental results, including the use of the relationships $q = mc\Delta T$ and $\Delta H = \dfrac{-mc\Delta T}{n}$
Prerequisites: 9701-5.1.3 Standard conditions and enthalpy change terms
Glossary: q=mcdeltat, specific heat capacity, temperature change, moles reacted
