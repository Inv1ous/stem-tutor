## KCs

### P2-5.1 — Exponential function y = a^x (conceptual)
Statement: $y=a^x$ and its graph.
Guidance: $a>0$, $a \neq 1$.
Prerequisites: P1-1.1 Laws of indices
Glossary: exponential function, y = a^x, exponential graph, base of an exponential

### P2-5.2 — Laws of logarithms (procedural)
Statement: Laws of logarithms.
Guidance: To include $\log_a(xy) \equiv \log_a x + \log_a y$, $\log_a\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right) \equiv \log_a x - \log_a y$, $\log_a(x^k) \equiv k\log_a x$, $\log_a\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right) \equiv -\log_a x$, $\log_a a = 1$, where $a,x,y>0$, $a \neq 1$.
Prerequisites: P2-5.1 Exponential function y = a^x
Glossary: laws of logarithms, log_a(xy), log_a(x/y), log_a(x^k), change of base

### P2-5.3 — Solving a^x = b (procedural)
Statement: The solution of equations of the form $a^x=b$.
Guidance: Students may use the change of base formula.
Prerequisites: P2-5.2 Laws of logarithms
Glossary: solving exponential equations, a^x = b, change of base formula, logarithms
