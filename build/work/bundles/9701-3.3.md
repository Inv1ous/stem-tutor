### 9701-3.3.1 — Definition of metallic bonding (factual)
Statement: define metallic bonding as the electrostatic attraction between positive metal ions and delocalised electrons
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.2 Charges and masses of subatomic particles
Glossary: metallic bonding, delocalised electrons, metal ion lattice
