## KCs

### 9702-5.2.1 — Deriving gravitational potential energy change (procedural)
Statement: derive, using $W = Fs$, the formula $\Delta E_P = mg\Delta h$ for gravitational potential energy changes in a uniform gravitational field
Prerequisites: 9702-5.1.1 Work done by a force; 9702-3.1.6 Weight as a gravitational force
Glossary: gravitational potential energy, work done against gravity, delta ep = mg delta h

### 9702-5.2.2 — Using gravitational potential energy change (procedural)
Statement: recall and use the formula $\Delta E_P = mg\Delta h$ for gravitational potential energy changes in a uniform gravitational field
Prerequisites: 9702-5.2.1 Deriving gravitational potential energy change
Glossary: gravitational potential energy, delta ep = mg delta h, potential energy change

### 9702-5.2.3 — Deriving kinetic energy formula (procedural)
Statement: derive, using the equations of motion, the formula for kinetic energy $E_K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
Prerequisites: 9702-2.1.6 Deriving the suvat equations; 9702-5.1.1 Work done by a force
Glossary: kinetic energy, ek = half mv squared, suvat equations

### 9702-5.2.4 — Using the kinetic energy formula (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $E_K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
Prerequisites: 9702-5.2.3 Deriving kinetic energy formula
Glossary: kinetic energy, ek = half mv squared, speed
