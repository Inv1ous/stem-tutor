## KCs

### S1-2.1 — Histograms, stem-and-leaf, box plots (procedural)
Statement: Histograms, stem and leaf diagrams, box plots.
Guidance: Using histograms, stem and leaf diagrams and box plots to compare distributions. Back-to-back stem and leaf diagrams may be required. Drawing of histograms, stem and leaf diagrams or box plots will not be the direct focus of examination questions.
Prerequisites: S1-1.1 Modelling in probability and statistics
Glossary: histogram, stem-and-leaf diagram, box plot, back-to-back stem-and-leaf diagram, frequency density

### S1-2.2 — Mean, median and mode (procedural)
Statement: Measures of location – mean, median, mode.
Guidance: Calculation of mean, mode and median, range and interquartile range will not be the direct focus of examination questions. Students will be expected to draw simple inferences and give interpretations to measures of location and dispersion. Significance tests will not be expected. Data may be discrete, continuous, grouped or ungrouped. Understanding and use of coding.
Prerequisites: S1-2.1 Histograms, stem-and-leaf, box plots
Glossary: mean, median, mode, coding, grouped data

### S1-2.3 — Variance, standard deviation, IQR (procedural)
Statement: Measures of dispersion – variance, standard deviation, range and interpercentile ranges.
Guidance: Simple interpolation may be required. Interpretation of measures of location and dispersion.
Prerequisites: S1-2.2 Mean, median and mode
Glossary: variance, standard deviation, range, interpercentile range, interquartile range, interpolation

### S1-2.4 — Skewness and outliers (conceptual)
Statement: Skewness. Concepts of outliers.
Guidance: Students may be asked to illustrate the location of outliers on a box plot. Any rule to identify outliers will be specified in the question.
Prerequisites: S1-2.3 Variance, standard deviation, IQR; S1-2.1 Histograms, stem-and-leaf, box plots
Glossary: skewness, positive skew, negative skew, outlier, box plot

## Command words

- **Show that**: Prove a given result; every step must be shown (answer given).
- **Hence**: Use the previous result to reach the answer.
- **Hence or otherwise**: Using the previous result is expected, but any valid method earns the marks.
- **Find**: Work out; method marks need working.
- **Calculate**: Work out from given facts; show working.
- **Prove**: Give a complete logical argument.
- **Sketch**: Draw the shape with key features labelled (intercepts, asymptotes, turning points), not to scale.
- **Exact**: Give the answer in surd, fraction, pi or ln form, not a decimal.
- **Verify**: Show that given values satisfy the statement.
- **State**: Give the answer without working.
- **Explain**: Give reasons in context.
- **Interpret**: Explain the meaning of a result in the context of the question.
