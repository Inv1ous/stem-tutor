## KCs

### 9702-8.3.1 — Interference and coherence (factual)
Statement: understand the terms interference and coherence
Prerequisites: 9702-8.1.1 Principle of superposition
Glossary: interference, coherence, phase relationship

### 9702-8.3.2 — Demonstrating two-source interference (procedural)
Statement: show an understanding of experiments that demonstrate two-source interference using water waves in a ripple tank, sound, light and microwaves
Prerequisites: 9702-8.3.1 Interference and coherence
Glossary: two-source interference, coherent sources, interference pattern

### 9702-8.3.3 — Conditions for interference fringes (conceptual)
Statement: understand the conditions required if two-source interference fringes are to be observed
Prerequisites: 9702-8.3.1 Interference and coherence; 9702-8.3.2 Demonstrating two-source interference
Glossary: coherence, interference fringes, path difference

### 9702-8.3.4 — Double-slit interference formula (procedural)
Statement: recall and use $\lambda = ax/D$ for double-slit interference using light
Prerequisites: 9702-8.3.3 Conditions for interference fringes; 9702-7.1.5 Using the wave equation, v = fλ
Glossary: double-slit interference, fringe spacing, lambda = ax/d
