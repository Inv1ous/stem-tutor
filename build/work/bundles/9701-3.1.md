Spec: CAIE AS & A Level Chemistry 9701 (2025-2027). Pack file: `build/out/packs/9701/9701-3.1.json`. Note path: `Subjects/9701 Chemistry/03 Chemical bonding/3.1 Electronegativity and bonding.md` (write it under `build/out/notes/`). Diagram files: `Assets/9701/9701-3.1-<name>.svg`.

## KCs

### 9701-3.1.1 — Definition of electronegativity (factual)
Statement: define electronegativity as the power of an atom to attract electrons to itself
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.3 Atomic, proton, mass and nucleon number
Glossary: electronegativity, attract electrons, pauling scale

### 9701-3.1.2 — Factors affecting electronegativity (conceptual)
Statement: explain the factors influencing the electronegativities of the elements in terms of nuclear charge, atomic radius and shielding by inner shells and sub-shells
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.1 Definition of electronegativity; 9701-1.4.6 Factors affecting ionisation energy
Glossary: nuclear charge, atomic radius, shielding, electronegativity

### 9701-3.1.3 — Trends in electronegativity (conceptual)
Statement: state and explain the trends in electronegativity across a period and down a group of the Periodic Table
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.2 Factors affecting electronegativity
Glossary: electronegativity trend, period trend, group trend

### 9701-3.1.4 — Predicting bond type from electronegativity difference (procedural)
Statement: use the differences in Pauling electronegativity values to predict the formation of ionic and covalent bonds (the presence of covalent character in some ionic compounds will not be assessed) (Pauling electronegativity values will be given where necessary)
Prerequisites: 9701-3.1.3 Trends in electronegativity
Glossary: pauling electronegativity, electronegativity difference, ionic bond, covalent bond
