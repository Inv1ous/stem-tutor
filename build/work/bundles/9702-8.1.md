## KCs

### 9702-8.1.1 — Principle of superposition (procedural)
Statement: explain and use the principle of superposition
Prerequisites: 9702-7.1.2 Wave terms: amplitude, period, wavelength
Glossary: principle of superposition, superposition of waves, resultant displacement

### 9702-8.1.2 — Demonstrating stationary waves (procedural)
Statement: show an understanding of experiments that demonstrate stationary waves using microwaves, stretched strings and air columns (it will be assumed that end corrections are negligible; knowledge of the concept of end corrections is not required)
Prerequisites: 9702-8.1.1 Principle of superposition
Glossary: stationary wave, microwaves, stretched strings, air columns

### 9702-8.1.3 — Formation of a stationary wave (conceptual)
Statement: explain the formation of a stationary wave using a graphical method, and identify nodes and antinodes
Prerequisites: 9702-8.1.2 Demonstrating stationary waves
Glossary: stationary wave, node, antinode, superposition

### 9702-8.1.4 — Wavelength from nodes and antinodes (procedural)
Statement: understand how wavelength may be determined from the positions of nodes or antinodes of a stationary wave
Prerequisites: 9702-8.1.3 Formation of a stationary wave; 9702-7.1.2 Wave terms: amplitude, period, wavelength
Glossary: node, antinode, wavelength measurement
