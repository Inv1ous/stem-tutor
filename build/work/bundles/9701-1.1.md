## KCs

### 9701-1.1.1 — Atomic structure: nucleus and electron shells (factual)
Statement: understand that atoms are mostly empty space surrounding a very small, dense nucleus that contains protons and neutrons; electrons are found in shells in the empty space around the nucleus
Prerequisites: none
Glossary: nucleus, proton, neutron, electron shell, empty space

### 9701-1.1.2 — Charges and masses of subatomic particles (factual)
Statement: identify and describe protons, neutrons and electrons in terms of their relative charges and relative masses
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.1 Atomic structure: nucleus and electron shells
Glossary: relative charge, relative mass, proton, neutron, electron

### 9701-1.1.3 — Atomic, proton, mass and nucleon number (factual)
Statement: understand the terms atomic and proton number; mass and nucleon number
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.2 Charges and masses of subatomic particles
Glossary: atomic number, proton number, mass number, nucleon number

### 9701-1.1.4 — Distribution of mass and charge in an atom (conceptual)
Statement: describe the distribution of mass and charge within an atom
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.1 Atomic structure: nucleus and electron shells; 9701-1.1.2 Charges and masses of subatomic particles
Glossary: mass distribution, charge distribution, nucleus, electron shell

### 9701-1.1.5 — Deflection of particle beams in an electric field (conceptual)
Statement: describe the behaviour of beams of protons, neutrons and electrons moving at the same velocity in an electric field
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.2 Charges and masses of subatomic particles
Glossary: electric field, deflection, charge to mass ratio, beam of particles

### 9701-1.1.6 — Counting protons, neutrons and electrons (procedural)
Statement: determine the numbers of protons, neutrons and electrons present in both atoms and ions given atomic or proton number, mass or nucleon number and charge
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.3 Atomic, proton, mass and nucleon number
Glossary: proton number, nucleon number, ion charge, number of neutrons, number of electrons

### 9701-1.1.7 — Trends in atomic and ionic radius (conceptual)
Statement: state and explain qualitatively the variations in atomic radius and ionic radius across a period and down a group
Prerequisites: 9701-1.1.3 Atomic, proton, mass and nucleon number
Glossary: atomic radius, ionic radius, period trend, group trend, nuclear charge
