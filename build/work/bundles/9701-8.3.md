## KCs

### 9701-8.3.1 — Catalysis and activation energy (conceptual)
Statement: explain and use the terms catalyst and catalysis: (a) explain that, in the presence of a catalyst, a reaction has a different mechanism, i.e. one of lower activation energy (b) explain this catalytic effect in terms of the Boltzmann distribution (c) construct and interpret a reaction pathway diagram, for a reaction in the presence and absence of an effective catalyst
Prerequisites: 9701-8.2.1 Definition of activation energy; 9701-5.1.2 Reaction pathway diagrams
Glossary: catalyst, catalysis, alternative mechanism, lower activation energy, reaction pathway diagram
