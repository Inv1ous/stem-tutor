The wave shows the electric field of monochromatic light at one instant. The labelled crest-to-crest distance $\lambda$ is one wavelength: this is the quantity determined using $d\sin\theta=n\lambda$. The sketch is schematic, with no numerical scale.

### Optional graph method
For the same monochromatic light and grating, measure $\theta$ for several orders. Plot order $n$ on the vertical axis against $\sin\theta$ on the horizontal axis. Rearranging the grating equation gives $n=(d/\lambda)\sin\theta$, so the expected relationship is a straight line through the origin. Draw a line of best fit and determine its gradient $G=\Delta n/\Delta(\sin\theta)$ using widely separated points on the line. Hence $G=d/\lambda$ and $\lambda=d/G$. Obtain $d=1/N$ from the line density $N$ in lines per metre; convert lines per millimetre first.
