## New lesson-note lines

> [!example]- Worked comparison: nitric acid has two roles
> For copper with dilute nitric acid, Cu rises from $0$ to $+2$, while N forming NO falls from $+5$ to $+2$. Three Cu atoms release six electrons; two nitrate N atoms gain those six electrons.
> Electron matching counts only the nitrate reduced to NO. Six more nitrate ions remain unchanged to supply the three $\ce{Cu(NO3)2}$ formula units. The acid coefficient is therefore $2+6=8$, not just the number reduced.
> $$
> \ce{3Cu(s) + 8HNO3(aq) -> 3Cu(NO3)2(aq) + 2NO(g) + 4H2O(l)}
> $$
> Check: each side has Cu 3, H 8, N 8 and O 24. The nitrate N that remains in the salt stays at $+5$.
