> [!example]- Worked example: prefixes in a quotient
> Convert $0.25\ \mathrm{kN\,mm^{-2}}$ to $\mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}$.
> Replace kilo in the numerator by $10^3$. In the denominator, square the whole millimetre conversion: $(10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m})^2=10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
> $0.25\ \mathrm{kN\,mm^{-2}}=\dfrac{0.25\times10^3\ \mathrm{N}}{(10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m})^2}=\dfrac{0.25\times10^3}{10^{-6}}\ \mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}=2.5\times10^8\ \mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}$.
> Dividing by $10^{-6}$ multiplies by $10^6$; combine this with the numerator's factor of $10^3$.
