*Figure: Hydroxide lone pair attacks a proton: follow the curly arrow from oxygen to hydrogen (source: 9701 c13.2, p.18)*
Notice that the red curly arrow starts at the oxygen lone pair and points to the hydrogen ion, showing where that electron pair forms a new bond.
**HCl–alkene arrow check:** Start the first full curly arrow at the middle of the $\ce{C=C}$ $\pi$ bond and point it to H in $\ce{H-Cl}$. At the same time, draw a second full curly arrow from the $\ce{H-Cl}$ bond to Cl: that bond pair moves to chlorine. Do not start the first arrow at HCl or at a bonded carbon atom; its tail must identify the alkene electron pair.
