## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9701/9701-9.1-ionic-radii.svg]]
Two separate decreasing series: the cations have two occupied shells, while the anions have three. The gap is a jump to larger ions, not a connecting trend through silicon; heights are schematic.
![[Assets/9701/9701-9.1-melting-trend.svg]]
Na–Al: strengthening metallic bonding; Si: the giant covalent peak; P–S–Cl: simple molecular substances, with stronger attractions between $\ce{S8}$ molecules than between $\ce{P4}$ molecules; Ar: monatomic, with very weak attractions between atoms. Heights show the qualitative pattern, not a numerical scale.
