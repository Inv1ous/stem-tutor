## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9701/9701-7.1-equilibrium-rates.svg]]
The graph shows reaction rates, not concentrations. From $t_e$ onwards, both reactions continue at equal, nonzero rates: concentrations stay constant but need not be equal. In a closed system at equilibrium under unchanged conditions, measurable properties such as colour or pressure also remain constant even though molecules continue reacting.
