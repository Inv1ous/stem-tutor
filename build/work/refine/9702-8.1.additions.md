## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9702/9702-8.1-stationary-snapshots.svg]]
The solid and dashed curves show opposite extreme displacements of the same stationary wave, half a period apart; they are not the two component waves. N marks fixed nodes at $0$, $\lambda/2$ and $\lambda$; A marks antinodes halfway between. The bracket spans $\lambda/4$ from a node to its nearest antinode. Node and antinode positions stay fixed while the string oscillates.
Sources for this diagram and the experimental practice: `sources/library/SaveMyExams/9702 a-level-physics-cie-25/9702 c8.1.pdf`, pp. 7–9. Additional detector procedure: [University of Illinois, Physics 401 Experiment 34](https://courses.physics.illinois.edu/phys401/fa2013/Files/Microwaves/E34_Spring09.pdf), pp. 10–11, explains the detector response to electric-field orientation and measuring successive signal minima.
