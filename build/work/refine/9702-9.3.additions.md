These are illustrative characteristics, not universal numerical values. The metal is straight through the origin; the lamp flattens symmetrically; the diode has negligible reverse current and a sharp forward rise.
> [!example]- Worked example: stretching at constant volume
> A uniform wire is stretched uniformly to three times its original length, keeping its volume and resistivity unchanged. Compare both resistances at the same temperature.
> First use $A_1L_1=A_2L_2$: since $L_2=3L_1$, $A_2=A_1/3$.
> Then $R_2/R_1=(L_2/L_1)(A_1/A_2)=3\times3=9$.
> The resistance becomes nine times as large. With fixed volume $\mathcal{V}=AL$, $R=\rho L^2/\mathcal{V}$, so $R\propto L^2$ for unchanged resistivity. Counting only the extra length misses the smaller area.
