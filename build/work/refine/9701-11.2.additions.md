## New lesson-note lines

> [!example]- Worked example: comparing thermal stability
> Compare HBr and HI on heating. The H–Br covalent bond is stronger than the H–I bond, so more energy is needed to break H–Br. HBr therefore resists decomposition more and is more thermally stable than HI.
> **Try the parallel comparison:** Which is more thermally stable, HCl or HBr? Compare H–Cl and H–Br bond strengths first, then connect bond breaking to decomposition.
