## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9701/9701-3.1-period-3-trend.svg]]
Notice the uninterrupted rise from Na to Cl. Heights and slopes are schematic, not exact Pauling values; increasing nuclear charge and decreasing radius strengthen attraction while inner-shell shielding changes little.
| Property | What it concerns | Period 3 pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Electronegativity | Attraction of an atom for electrons in a bond. | Generally rises from Na to Cl. |
| First ionisation energy | Energy required to remove an electron from a gaseous atom. | Generally rises, but has dips from Mg to Al and from P to S. |
The first-ionisation-energy dip from Mg to Al reflects removal of a higher-energy, more shielded $3p$ electron rather than a $3s$ electron. The P to S dip reflects repulsion between paired electrons in a $3p$ orbital, making one easier to remove. These electron-removal effects must not be transferred mechanically to a sketch of electronegativity.
