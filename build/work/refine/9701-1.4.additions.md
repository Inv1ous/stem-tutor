## New lesson-note lines

The same two features appear in Period 2, and the start of each new period shows the group trend:
![[Assets/9701/9701-1.4-h-to-ne-ie1.svg]]
Notice the huge drop from He to Li: the outer electron of Li is in a new shell, further from the nucleus and shielded by the full $1s$ shell, which is exactly why $IE_1$ falls down a group.
