## New lesson-note lines

> [!example]- Worked example: abundance weighting
> A sample contains only two isotopes with relative isotopic masses 34.97 and 36.97, at abundances of 75% and 25% respectively. Calculate its $A_r$.
> First check that $75+25=100\%$: all atoms are accounted for. Weight each mass by its share of the atoms, so the more abundant isotope contributes more.
> $A_r=(34.97\times75+36.97\times25)/100=35.47$.
> This unitless mean lies between the two isotope masses and nearer 34.97, the more abundant isotope. Simply averaging the two masses would incorrectly assume equal abundances.
> **Try with less guidance:** Use the same isotope masses with abundances 60% and 40%. The total is 100%; complete the weighted mean yourself.
> [!answer]- Check the faded example
> $A_r=(34.97\times60+36.97\times40)/100=35.77$, with no unit.
