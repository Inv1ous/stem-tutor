## New lesson-note lines

**Measuring p.d.:** To measure the p.d. across a lamp, connect a voltmeter in parallel with that lamp: one terminal at each side. Its terminals compare the potentials of those two connection points, so the reading refers to energy transferred per unit charge across the named lamp.
