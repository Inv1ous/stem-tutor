## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9702/9702-8.3-intensity-fringes.svg]]
This idealised profile shows the central part of a two-slit pattern for sources emitting in phase, neglecting the variation of the diffraction envelope. Peaks mark bright fringes; the marked interval $x$ joins adjacent bright peaks. Equal amplitudes of the two arriving waves allow zero-intensity minima where they arrive in antiphase.
> [!example]- Counting dark fringes
> For sources emitting in phase, start at the central maximum: the first dark fringe has path difference $\lambda/2$, and the second has $3\lambda/2$. For example, if $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$, these are $300\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $900\,\mathrm{nm}$.
> The fringe spacing $x$ is the distance along the screen between adjacent fringes of the same kind (bright to bright or dark to dark); it is not itself a path difference between the two light paths.
