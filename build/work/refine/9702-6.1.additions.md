## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9702/9702-6.1-stress-strain-gradient.svg]]
The triangle gives $E=\Delta\sigma/\Delta\varepsilon$ only in the proportional region. The short curved continuation shows loss of proportionality; its endpoint is not an identified elastic limit.
Repeat each extension reading and average to reduce random error; after each reading, unload and check that the wire returns to its initial reading. Exclude data outside the proportional region when finding $E$, even if the wire recovers on unloading.
*Sources for these additions: Save My Exams, `sources/library/SaveMyExams/9702 a-level-physics-cie-25/9702 c6.1.pdf`, pp.10–13 (gradient and practical checks); [Government College of Engineering Bakhtiyarpur physics laboratory manual](https://gcekbpatna.ac.in/assets/documents/lecturenotes/Lab_Manual-PHYSICS.pdf), PDF pp.14–16 (reference-wire apparatus and loading/unloading measurements).*
