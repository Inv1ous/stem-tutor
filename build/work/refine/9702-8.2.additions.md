Notice how the outgoing crests become more curved as the gap narrows; measure crest spacing along the direction of wave travel.

The marked crest-to-crest distance is the wavelength $\lambda$ to compare with gap width in the ripple-tank figure. This displacement-distance graph shows a side profile at one instant; the ripple-tank photographs show the crests from above. Both views represent the same wavelength. See [OpenStax, wavefront and side-profile comparison](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-ap-courses-2e/pages/27-2-huygenss-principle-diffraction).
