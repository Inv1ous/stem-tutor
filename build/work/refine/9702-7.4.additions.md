## New lesson-note lines

![[Assets/9702/9702-7.4-transverse-wave.svg]]
This snapshot shows the electric field oscillating up and down, perpendicular to propagation to the right; the curve is not the path of a particle. The labelled $\lambda$ is the distance between successive crests: different electromagnetic regions have different wavelength ranges (visible light is about 400–700 nm), but all retain speed $c$ in free space.
*Sources: learner's library, `sources/library/SaveMyExams/9702 a-level-physics-cie-25/9702 c7.4.pdf`, pp. 2–4 (shared speed and wavelength–frequency relationship); [OpenStax, University Physics Volume 2, §16.2](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/16-2-plane-electromagnetic-waves) (electric-field orientation for the schematic).*
