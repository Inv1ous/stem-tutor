- `refine/refine.py`:
  - `ensure_candidate` also prepares `candidate_teach`. New `teach_path(sub)`.
  - `gates(sub, defer_written=False)` passes `candidate_teach` and `defer_written` to `fd.gates`. With deferral,
    written-review problems are counted but do not fail. Signing never defers.
  - `cmd_collect`: the refiner and fixer use deferred gates. The fixer goes to `recheck` when written reviews are pending, or when a
    `.check` / `.retry` ref was fixed.
  - New written-review flow:
    - Helpers: `written_items`, `pending_written`, `_written_questions`, `_written_answers`, `_written_jobs`,
      `write_written_items`, `bind_written`, `_ingest_written`.
    - `cmd_strip` writes `build/work/blind/<sub>.refine.written.questions.json` (stems only).
    - `todo` / `cmd_dispatch` run `wsolver`, then `wreviewer`. `cmd_dispatch` writes `build/work/refine/<sub>.written-items.json`.
    - `cmd_compare` (solve/recheck) ingests `<sub>.written-decisions.json`. Accepted decisions go to `<sub>.written-review.json`
      through `build_records`. Rejected or missing decisions become open findings with rule `written review rejected`.
