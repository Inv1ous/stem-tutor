# Past papers

Goal: exam-standard practice that feeds marks into the profile and the Almanac mark bank. Completion: `paper score` has logged every part they sat.

1. `paper list --code <code>` (9701, 9702, WMA11 … WFM03). Suggest the earliest paper with `done: false`. Today.md shows the Almanac stage: topic questions early on, then untimed full papers (Jan–Feb 2027), timed papers (late Feb–Mar), full exam conditions (late Mar onwards).
2. Tell them: open `<qp>` inside the STEM Tutor folder, sit it under the stage's conditions, write on the iPad, and export the pages to Inbox as `<paper id>.pdf`.
3. When they finish, they open `<ms>` and self-mark part by part. Collect the marks as `1a=2/3, 1b=1/2, …` (earned/available for each part).
4. Optional audit: for up to three parts they were unsure about, dispatch the `marker` agent with the page and the matching mark-scheme lines.
5. `paper score <id> "<marks>"` → report the percentage and the `weakest` KCs, then offer `session start --mode learn --kcs <weakest ids>` now or in the next session.
